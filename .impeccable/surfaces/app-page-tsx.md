# Surface: OniStore home and product pages

Scope: `/` (home) and `/producto/[slug]`. Visitor mode: Persuade. Admin (`/admin`) is out of scope and keeps its own Operate styling.

Audience: adults who want to sleep better, mothers buying for daughters, couples buying matching sets; arriving from Instagram/TikTok on a phone, at night. Action: pick a pajama, size and colour, send the order by WhatsApp. Proof on hand: none beyond the products themselves; no photos yet (illustrations stand in, labelled as such in the report), no testimonials. Constraints: palette pinned by the owner; prices in USD; admin-managed content.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a chromatic-interference floor in the tradition of Venezuelan kinetic art: thin lamellae in the seven brand colours that blend optically into the sunset and turn toward night as the visitor moves. It refuses the soft pajama-shop page (cream ground, serif, moon icons, centred hero over a card row).

OWN-WORLD: Medianoche ground at full strength. Colour lives in lamellae fields: hard-edged vertical slats, two faces per slat plus a thin core line, sequenced gold, coral, magenta, plum, night. Type: a variable-width grotesk (Anybody) whose width answers to motion, with Figtree for reading. Corners square on fields, round only on controls. Reveals open like slats turning.

STORY: The visitor sees "Duerme bonito" cut through the moving floor, picks tonight's pajama in its colours, browses a collection where every piece stands on its own floor, plans the night in 90-minute bands, and sends the order on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: night bar with mark, links, bag. Left seven columns: the lamellae field with "Duerme / bonito" knocked out of it at display scale and one supporting line beneath. Right five columns: tonight's pajama on its plate, price, colour swatches that retune the whole floor, sizes, a working "Añadir a la bolsa". Phone: title across the width, product and add button inside the first screen.

FORM: Cromointerferencia, second on the ordered list, taken as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the assigned dream-dictionary direction. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
